# Design decisions for revision 2

The supplied Half-Life, Portal and The Last of Us menu references share a useful hierarchy: the world provides the atmosphere; the title establishes identity; a small set of choices remains easy to scan. The revision applies that hierarchy without copying those games' assets or logos.

The title and navigation use a single restrained condensed face. Wide title tracking and narrower option tracking establish two levels without a second display font. The main screen has no supporting slogans or status labels. An arrow is the only selection ornament. The interface remains recognizably a game menu rather than an editorial page or scene slideshow.

The night scenes use strong ink blacks and a cool blue shadow ramp. Whites stay neutral instead of olive. Amber is tied to actual task lights, electrical hardware and ignition, which gives warm/cool contrast a physical source. Mars uses its own earth-color ramp within the same palette. No global hue overlays, grain, softness or bloom are layered over the artwork.

Submenus reuse the same type and colors, with small rounded edges and simple spacing. Their appearance, departure and internal view changes are animated; the scene remains visible and sharp behind them. Destination images replace decorative gradient balls. Copy describes decisions rather than decorating the screen.

The [SkullKeys menu-design reference](https://skullkeys.com/how-to-design-a-game-main-menu/) informed the emphasis on a simple menu, a shared visual language and movement tied to the world. The supplied Disco Elysium image informed environmental density, material detail and value composition; the deliverable retains the project's pixel-art requirement rather than copying that game's rendering style.

The new palette and condensed font intentionally take precedence over the asset brief's suggested Endesga palette and optional pixel heading font. The brief still guides native-size images, sharp grids, shared suit construction, limited color roles, no baked labels, no real-agency logos and documented asset provenance.
