# Character direction

The roster remains eight different fictional people in one practical ivory pressure-suit family. Age, face, hair, glasses/headsets, height, shoulder/hip dimensions and professional gestures carry identity. There are no decorative armor spikes, exaggerated waists or gender-specific suit technology.

Ayesha has a greying low bun; Maya a short crop and compact broad build; Bilal is tall/lean with a headset; Lin has a neat bob/headset; Sana has tied waves and round glasses; Kofi has close textured hair and beard; Omar has grey hair/beard and a stockier body; Zara has an auburn braid. New directional head artwork preserves those identities from front and back. Rear views show actual scalp/hair/nape rather than recolored faces.

Crew suits use ivory fabric/shells, dark joint seals, practical gloves/boots, a compact three-dimensional pack and chest controls. Amber, blue, teal and muted violet patches identify roles. The separate main player retains the more distinctive navy/ivory/copper chevron design.

The shared 64-color palette continues the parent menu's restrained night palette. There is no runtime blur, grading, grain, bloom or color shift. Sprite pixels are opaque or transparent; small SVGs use integer pixel paths. The original concept and new head sheets are credited AI-assisted art, followed by explicit alpha/palette/grid cleanup and a code-defined solid rig.

The standard/gameplay sizes remain compatible. Detail exports support close facial inspection; 16 × 24 naturally communicates silhouette/role more than eye detail. Human heights are fictional design choices and are baked into the sprite set at a shared scale.
